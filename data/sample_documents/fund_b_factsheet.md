# Harbor Value Fund Factsheet

Fund ID: FUND_B
Source Type: fund_fact_sheet
As of: 2026-03-31

## Strategy

Harbor Value Fund seeks long-term total return by investing in U.S. companies that appear undervalued relative to earnings, cash flow, assets, or dividend potential. The portfolio emphasizes profitable businesses with durable balance sheets, shareholder returns, and identifiable catalysts for valuation improvement.

## Performance Notes

The fund returned 9.1% over the latest one-year period and 7.4% annualized over three years. Performance benefited from holdings in financials, industrials, and dividend-paying companies. The fund may trail growth-oriented peers when high-multiple technology and innovation stocks lead the market.

## Risk Notes

The fund has volatility of 12.8%, lower than the growth fund in this sample but still exposed to equity market risk. Value strategies can underperform for extended periods when cheap companies remain out of favor or when earnings expectations deteriorate.

## Fees and Expenses

The expense ratio is 0.52%. The fund's fee profile is below the growth fund and close to the balanced allocation fund in this sample.

## Portfolio Exposure

The portfolio is primarily invested in U.S. equities with a value orientation. It may include exposure to financials, energy, industrials, health care, and established consumer companies. Fixed income exposure is not a primary part of the strategy.

## Analyst Notes

Harbor Value Fund may be suitable for investors who want equity exposure with a valuation discipline and somewhat lower volatility than aggressive growth funds. It can complement a growth allocation in a diversified portfolio.
