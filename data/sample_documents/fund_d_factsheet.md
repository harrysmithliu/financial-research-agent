# Riverstone Balanced Allocation Fund Factsheet

Fund ID: FUND_D
Source Type: fund_fact_sheet
As of: 2026-03-31

## Strategy

Riverstone Balanced Allocation Fund seeks balanced growth and income by investing across equities, fixed income, and cash equivalents. The fund uses a multi-asset allocation framework intended to reduce reliance on a single return source while maintaining participation in market upside.

## Performance Notes

The fund returned 7.8% over the latest one-year period and 6.1% annualized over three years. Equity exposure contributed to returns, while bond exposure helped moderate volatility. The fund may underperform pure equity funds during strong bull markets but may provide a smoother return pattern.

## Risk Notes

The fund has volatility of 9.7%, placing it between the equity funds and the global bond fund in this sample. Asset allocation decisions, interest-rate changes, equity market declines, and credit conditions can all affect performance.

## Fees and Expenses

The expense ratio is 0.58%. The fee reflects active allocation across multiple asset classes and is higher than the global bond fund but lower than the growth fund.

## Portfolio Exposure

The portfolio generally includes U.S. and international equities, investment-grade bonds, and short-term instruments. Allocation ranges may change based on valuation, macroeconomic trends, and risk conditions.

## Analyst Notes

Riverstone Balanced Allocation Fund may be suitable for investors seeking a diversified core allocation with moderate risk. It can serve as a reference point when comparing more aggressive equity funds or more conservative income funds.
