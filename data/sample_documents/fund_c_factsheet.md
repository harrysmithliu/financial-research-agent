# Summit Global Bond Fund Factsheet

Fund ID: FUND_C
Source Type: fund_fact_sheet
As of: 2026-03-31

## Strategy

Summit Global Bond Fund seeks income and moderate total return by investing in a diversified portfolio of government, investment-grade corporate, securitized, and select international bonds. The strategy emphasizes income generation, credit quality, duration management, and currency risk controls.

## Performance Notes

The fund returned 5.2% over the latest one-year period and 3.6% annualized over three years. Returns were supported by coupon income and stable credit spreads. The fund is not designed to match the return potential of equity growth strategies during strong stock markets.

## Risk Notes

The fund has volatility of 6.4%, the lowest volatility among the four sample funds. Key risks include interest-rate risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, and foreign currency exposure. Bond prices can decline when yields rise or when credit conditions weaken.

## Fees and Expenses

The expense ratio is 0.38%, the lowest in the sample set. Lower expenses support income retention, especially in lower-return fixed income environments.

## Portfolio Exposure

The portfolio is globally diversified across fixed income sectors. It typically holds limited equity exposure and may use currency hedging where appropriate. Duration and credit exposure are adjusted based on macroeconomic conditions.

## Analyst Notes

Summit Global Bond Fund may be suitable for investors seeking income, lower volatility, and diversification away from equity market risk. It is not risk-free and should be evaluated in the context of interest-rate and credit conditions.
