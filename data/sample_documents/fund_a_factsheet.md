# Northstar Growth Fund Factsheet

Fund ID: FUND_A
Source Type: fund_fact_sheet
As of: 2026-03-31

## Strategy

Northstar Growth Fund seeks long-term capital appreciation by investing primarily in large and mid-cap U.S. companies with above-average revenue growth, expanding margins, and durable competitive advantages. The portfolio emphasizes technology, health care innovation, digital services, and select consumer growth businesses.

## Performance Notes

The fund returned 12.4% over the latest one-year period and 8.9% annualized over three years. Recent performance was supported by exposure to software, semiconductor, and medical technology companies. The fund may lag during periods when investors favor defensive sectors or lower-valuation equities.

## Risk Notes

The fund has a higher volatility profile than the broader balanced sample set, with volatility of 15.2%. Growth stocks may be sensitive to interest-rate expectations, earnings revisions, and valuation compression. Investors should expect larger drawdowns than lower-risk bond or balanced allocation strategies.

## Fees and Expenses

The expense ratio is 0.65%. The fee level is higher than the income-oriented bond fund in this sample, but broadly aligned with actively managed growth equity strategies.

## Portfolio Exposure

The portfolio is concentrated in U.S. equity growth sectors. It generally has limited direct exposure to fixed income, cash equivalents, or international bonds. Sector weights may shift based on earnings momentum and valuation discipline.

## Analyst Notes

Northstar Growth Fund may be suitable as a satellite equity allocation for investors seeking growth exposure and willing to accept elevated volatility. It should not be treated as a complete portfolio on its own.
